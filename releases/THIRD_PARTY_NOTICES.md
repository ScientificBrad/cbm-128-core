## 1. Clean Distribution Policy

This repository contains an independent, clean-room software reconstruction, architectural emulator, and reverse-engineered analytical ledger.

- **No Proprietary Firmware:** Proprietary Commodore ROM binary images (`basic-4000.318018-02.bin`, `basic-8000.318019-02.bin`, `kernal.318020-03.bin`, `characters.390059-01.bin`, `z80bios.bin`) are **excluded** from this repository and automated release artifacts. Users must dump and provide their own firmware binaries.
- **No Commercial Software:** Proprietary floppy disk images (`.d64`, `.d71`, `.d81`) and commercial software titles (e.g. *Kikstart 128*, *CP/M Plus 3.0*) are **excluded** from this repository.
